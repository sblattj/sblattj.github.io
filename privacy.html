Privacy policy

This OAuth client is for personal use by its owner only. It is not offered to other users.

  • Google user data accessed through it (Gmail, Calendar, Drive and related Workspace data) is used only to perform actions the owner asks their own tools to perform.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machines. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
